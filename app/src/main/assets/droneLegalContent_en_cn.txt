Aircraft Disclaimer and Legal Information

1. Confirm that local laws allow drone flights before takeoff; stay away from crowds, airports and no-fly zones;
2. Fly within visual line of sight and avoid severe weather such as strong wind, rain or snow;
3. Check battery and propeller installation, and confirm compass/IMU calibration before takeoff;
4. Return or land immediately on low-battery warning to avoid crashes, property damage or injury;
5. The operator bears full legal responsibility for operating the aircraft.

This open-source rewrite keeps the informational text only, for interoperability research.
